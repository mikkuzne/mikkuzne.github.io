New preprint: [**On the Effectiveness of Kernel-Level Evidence for Agent Security**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28915). We introduce ACE, a 4,047-session corpus pairing kernel syscall traces with agent transcripts, and show that kernel evidence is discriminative on its own and complements application-layer signals.
